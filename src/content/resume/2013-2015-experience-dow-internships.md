**2013 – 2015 · Three nonconsecutive internship tours**

Completed three software engineering tours with the Department of War contributing to speech recognition research, radio frequency analysis, and data dissemination systems.

#### Key Accomplishments

- **Speech Recognition Services (June–August 2015):** Containerized **Kaldi online-decoding services** and built a **Flask HTTP backend** for speech-to-text research involving online decoding and forced alignment.
- **RF Testing & Data Collection (June–October 2014):** Developed a **.NET package integrating the VSA 89600 API** for instrument configuration, signal demodulation, data analysis and storage. Implemented peer-to-peer communication and UDP data transfers, and built a vulnerability-testing, collection, and tagging workflow for devices producing **RF signals up to 4 GHz**, including applicable Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and GSM devices.
- **Data Dissemination Systems (March–August 2013):** Extended application functionality using **Google Web Toolkit and Python**. Tested and refined data propagation tools with a focus on speed, precision, and user adoption, applying **agile development practices** to incorporate customer needs into iterative improvements.
